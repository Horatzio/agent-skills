# Skillbook: System 1 models for fast reviewers and classifiers

Skills for operating small, fast, cheap models (Haiku-class, GPT-mini-class, Gemini Flash-class, small open models) as reviewers and classifiers — prompting and structured outputs, review and classification harnesses, evaluation and calibration, routing and escalation, scale, and guardrails.
